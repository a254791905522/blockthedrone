Block the drone

Block the drone is a fast-paced arcade defense shooter. Move your hero, fire rockets, and stop incoming drone waves before they break through.

Game Highlights

  • 5 chapters and 50 levels with gradually increasing difficulty
  • Distinct chapter themes and chapter-based background music
  • Cartoon-style visuals with stronger enemy drone designs in later chapters
  • Simple controls: drag to move, auto-fire rockets
